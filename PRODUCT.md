# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Pingo is for a private lender or small ledger owner who needs to record clients,
debts, installments, and payments quickly from desktop or phone. The user may work
with unreliable connectivity and expects financial records to remain available on
the phone at all times.

## Product Purpose

Pingo provides one trustworthy debt ledger across an authenticated desktop web
application and the offline-first Dividas/Pingo mobile application. Success means
the user can understand who owes what, record or reverse payments confidently,
inspect an audit trail, receive reminders, and synchronize without losing offline
data or exposing one account to another.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, and private. Pingo should feel like a focused professional ledger:
confident without looking corporate, contemporary without becoming decorative,
and dense without feeling cramped.

## Anti-references

- SaaS marketing pages inside the authenticated product
- Blue-and-purple generic fintech dashboards
- Beige editorial layouts or paper metaphors
- Glassmorphism, gradient text, decorative orbs, and ambient visual noise
- Oversized typography, excessive rounded cards, or card grids used as structure
- Tiny uppercase eyebrow labels repeated above page titles
- Novel controls where familiar tables, menus, dialogs, sheets, and inputs work

## Design Principles

1. Put the ledger first. Every screen should make balances, status, dates, and the
   next available action easy to scan.
2. Make risk visible. Paid, unpaid, overdue, destructive, and irreversible states
   must never depend on color alone.
3. Preserve context. Mutations happen close to the record and return the user to a
   refreshed, predictable view.
4. Keep one vocabulary. The same action, status, control, and loading state should
   look and behave the same everywhere.
5. Design for interrupted connectivity. Mobile screens remain useful offline and
   communicate sync state without blocking ledger work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for text, controls, focus indication, keyboard operation, and
touch targets. Respect reduced motion, provide text or icon-plus-text status cues,
keep focus visible, and ensure desktop tables have usable mobile list equivalents.
Financial values must use clear formatting and must not be abbreviated in ways
that can change their meaning.
